# Company Overview

## What This Covers
What the business does, how it makes money, and the unit of value it counts most.
Drafted from the company's public site (https://nodal-data.github.io/shorelane/),
then confirmed by an analyst.

## Business model
<!-- status: confirmed — drafted from public site, analyst reviewed 2026-07-25 -->
Shorelane is an office-supplies commerce company (paper, ink & toner, furniture,
tech accessories, breakroom). It serves three segments: individual consumers,
business customers (small teams up to multi-location enterprises), and
third-party sellers who list on its marketplace while Shorelane handles
fulfillment.

## Revenue model
<!-- status: confirmed — drafted from public site, analyst reviewed 2026-07-25 -->
Three channels, with different cash and recognition timing:

1. **Direct-to-consumer (d2c)** — consumers pay up front at order time.
2. **Business subscriptions (Shorelane for Business)** — billed up front on
   net-30 invoicing; cash is collected later (and sometimes never — bad debt).
3. **Marketplace** — third-party sellers list products; Shorelane earns a
   per-sale commission by category, not the full ticket.

## Core unit of value
<!-- status: confirmed — analyst confirmed 2026-07-25 -->
The **order**. All three channels (d2c, business subscription, marketplace) flow
through orders; the order is the atom of value. Note that revenue *reporting*
happens on a long measure table (one row per date × measure), but the business
counts orders.
