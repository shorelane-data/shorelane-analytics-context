# Catalog-Margin — Context

<!-- status: draft — simulated-analyst answers; not human-confirmed. -->

Shorelane sells six office-supply categories, each from a single supplier.
Direct-to-consumer lines are Shorelane's own inventory, bought at a unit cost and
sold at list price less any promo discount — that is where gross margin lives.
Marketplace lines are third-party goods sold at the seller's retail price;
Shorelane earns only its commission on them, so they have no cost basis and are
excluded from margin. Business subscriptions are seat licences with no order
lines at all.
